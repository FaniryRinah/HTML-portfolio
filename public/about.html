About Me

Who I am ?

I’m Faniry, a data analyst and economist based in Réunion Island, France. For close to 3 years, I’ve been turning raw data into dashboards, forecasts, and reports that help businesses make better decisions. I currently work in the insurance sector, where I build the monthly executive committee report and support business teams with the numbers they need to understand their activity.

What do I do Day to day ?

I work with Power BI to design and automate dashboards, SQL to extract and query data, and R and Python for statistical analysis and forecasting. I also run monthly variance analysis to reconcile data and make sure the numbers I present can be trusted. I hold two Master’s degrees in Applied Economics, which gave me a strong foundation in econometrics and quantitative methods that I apply directly in my data work.

Why web development Right now ?

I’m learning web development from scratch, because I want to understand how the tools and dashboards I build for others actually come to life on the web. It’s a new challenge outside of data analysis, but the same curiosity that pushes me to dig into a dataset until I understand it is what’s driving me here too. This portfolio is where I’m documenting that journey, one project at a time.
